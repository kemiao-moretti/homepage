Saw [Dayu](https://anotherdayu.com/2023/5452/)'s post and the [App Defaults](https://defaults.rknight.me/) trend, so I figured I would share what I actually use day to day.

---

### Mail Client

- [Spark](https://sparkmailapp.com/) (Paid) - Multi-device sync is my favorite feature.

### Mail Server

- [iCloud](https://www.apple.com/icloud/) (Paid) - Custom domain email on iCloud+ is cheap and just works.
- [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/) - Inbound routing only. If you want a full setup, check out [Lepture](https://lepture.com/zh/2023/free-email-provider)'s write-up; for outbound delivery, [Resend](https://resend.com) is great.

### Notes

- [Apple Notes](https://www.icloud.com/notes) - Quick scratchpad ideas.
- [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/) - Work notes. Company security policies forbid cloud syncing, so it stays local.

### To-Do

- [Apple Reminders](https://www.icloud.com/reminders)

### iPhone Photo Shooting

- Apple Camera

### Photo Management

- [iCloud](https://www.apple.com/icloud/) (Paid)

### Calendar

- [Apple Calendar](https://www.icloud.com/calendar)

### Cloud File Storage

- [iCloud](https://www.apple.com/icloud/) (Paid) - Photo and document backups.
- [openmediavault](https://www.openmediavault.org/) - Movies, TV shows, and PT downloads.

### RSS

- [Inoreader](https://www.inoreader.com/) - The free plan with 150 feeds is plenty. Keeping around 100 high-quality feeds gives me plenty to read without wasting hours.
- [NetNewsWire](https://netnewswire.com/) - Free and clean RSS reader on macOS, paired with Inoreader.

### Contacts

- Apple Contacts

### Browser

- [Edge](https://www.microsoft.com/edge) - History syncs across all my machines. The [Better History](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/better-history/egehpkpgpgooebopjihjmnpejnjafefi) extension makes the history UI look way better.

### Chat

- [WeChat](https://www.wechat.com/) - The inescapable "small and beautiful."
- [Telegram](https://telegram.org/)

### Bookmarks

- [Browser Favorites](edge://favorites/)

### News

- [Twitter / X](https://404.li/twitter)
- [Mastodon](https://404.li/mastodon)

### Music

- [Apple Music](https://music.apple.com/) (Paid) - For tracks missing on QQ Music.
- [QQ Music](https://y.qq.com/) (Paid) - Most complete Chinese music catalog.

### Podcasts

- [Google Podcasts](https://podcasts.google.com/) - Shutting down soon, sadly. Still hunting for a replacement.

### Password Management

- [Enpass](https://www.enpass.io/) (Paid) - Bought a lifetime license for $30 eight years ago; should easily last me another eight.
